About This Model

The Fire Disaster Scenario model provides a visual representation of how fire spreads through structures and outdoor areas. It highlights critical elements including:

  • Fire spread patterns and hazard zones
  • Safe evacuation routes and assembly points
  • Critical infrastructure and emergency access
  • Safety equipment locations

Scale: 1 unit = 1 meter (models appear at realistic size in AR)
