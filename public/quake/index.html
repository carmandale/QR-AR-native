About This Model

The Earthquake Disaster Scenario model demonstrates the effects of seismic activity on buildings and infrastructure. Important elements include:

  • Structural damage patterns and collapse zones
  • Building vulnerabilities and safe areas
  • Emergency assembly points and shelter locations
  • Critical infrastructure and utility impacts

Scale: 1 unit = 1 meter (models appear at realistic size in AR)
